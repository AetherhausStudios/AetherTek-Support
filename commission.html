How It Works
📋
Submit a Brief
Describe what you need, your rough timeline, and how to reach you. The more detail, the more accurate Aether's response. Submitting a brief carries no obligation to proceed on either side.
💬
Discussion & Scope
Aether will reach out via Discord to fully understand the project before discussing cost. No payment is expected or requested until scope and cost have been discussed and agreed upon by both parties.
🤝
Negotiation is Welcome
Negotiation is not just tolerated — it is actively encouraged. If a quoted cost presents a genuine barrier, say so. The goal is to find an arrangement that works for both parties without placing undue strain on the client. Respectful discussion is always met with equal respect.
🔨
The Build
Once scope and cost are agreed, Aether builds. You'll be kept informed throughout. Aether reserves the right to decline — this will always be explained clearly. Common reasons: current work volume, project scope, or insufficient clarity. Declining is never personal.
