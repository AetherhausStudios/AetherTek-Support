⊹ Changelog ⊹

A running record of what's changed, improved, or been added to AetherTek and Hauscall. No version numbers, no patch notes — just honest updates on what's new and why.

Jul 2026
Hauscall Support Site
Partnership Network Live Alerts
When a watched streamer goes live in any partner community, Hauscall now posts a live alert to the AetherTek Support Hub's #partnership-network channel. Each alert includes the streamer's avatar, current game, viewer count, stream link, and an embed color matching their Discord role. This keeps all partners immediately informed when anyone in the network is streaming live.
Jul 2026
Support Site
Hardware Deals Auto-Posting
The software recommendations channel now receives automatic hardware deals posts from r/buildapcsales RSS feed. A new deal posts approximately once per hour, randomized to feel natural. Categories rotate daily (GPU → CPU → RAM → SSD → PSU → Monitor → Peripherals) and each deal is displayed in a color-coded embed with the post title, link, and category. The system uses persistent message editing to maintain a clean, organized feed without spam.
Jul 2026
Support Site
Software Guide Embeds
The software recommendations channel now displays five individual embeds for essential tools: Spybot Search & Destroy, Glary Utilities, Malwarebytes, HWiNFO, and Autoruns (Sysinternals). Each embed includes official download links, current version, cost, size, update frequency, and feature descriptions. Hauscall automatically checks for new versions every Sunday at 2 AM UTC and updates embeds in-place if versions have changed. All embeds include direct download links and safe usage guidance.
Jul 2026
Hauscall Moderation
Sentinel — Dark Topic Detection Expanded
Discord monitoring now detects a significantly broader range of dark and sensitive topics requiring mod attention: animal abuse and death (pet loss messages, abuse disclosures), graphic violence and injury descriptions, extreme grief and trauma (loss of loved ones, personal tragedy), and abuse/trauma disclosures. These trigger welfare concern alerts (💙) with a note that gentle mod check-ins may be appropriate. Detection is tuned to mid-sensitivity — catching concerning content without false-positives on every mention of sadness.
Jul 2026
Hauscall Moderation
YouTube Links — Self-Promotion Exemption
YouTube links are now exempt from self-promotion flagging. Members can share YouTube videos freely in monitored channels without triggering alerts. Other platforms (Twitch, TikTok, Instagram) remain subject to self-promotion detection where applicable. This change recognizes that video sharing is a legitimate form of communication and knowledge-sharing in communities.
Jul 2026
Hauscall Moderation
Throne Tracking — Data Integrity Fix
Throne contribution tracking has been removed from public-facing reports and Discord transcripts. The previous system flagged every mention of "throne" keyword, which created corrupted data since streamer posts of their Throne link were counted as contributions. Internal tracking is still maintained, but public Discord embeds and mod reports no longer display Throne data. This ensures post-stream reports reflect only verified community support metrics (bits, subs, donations).
June 2026
Jun 2026
Hauscall Security
Sentinel — Expanded Threat Detection
Hauscall's Discord monitor now detects a significantly broader range of threat patterns: phishing and scam links, the "Accidental Report" social engineering scam, Honeypot casino and withdrawal scams, item giveaway scams (with image attachment detection firing as confirmed), and unsolicited self-promotion. Detection runs in priority order — social engineering first, phishing second, honeypot third — so the most serious threats are always caught before anything else.
Jun 2026
Hauscall Security
Sentinel — Live Stream Mode & Stream Start Member Scan
When a streamer goes live, Hauscall automatically scans all Discord members who joined in the last 7 days and posts a summary to the mod alert channel — flagged accounts with score, account age, join date, and the signals that triggered the flag. From stream start to stream end, Hauscall enters live mode: any new join is scored against a stricter threshold since bad actors tend to time their activity for when streamers and mods are occupied. Live mode reverts automatically when the stream ends.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
Sentinel — High Priority Channels
Discord server configs now support a High Priority Channels list alongside the existing Watched Channels list. Messages in high priority channels receive a full scan across all threat categories and alert on both confirmed and review-severity detections — not just confirmed. Designed for general chat, welcome channels, and anywhere scam activity has been observed. Configurable per server from the AetherhausDesk Hub.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
Sentinel — New Account Scoring Tightened
Suspicion scoring for new joins has been tightened. Accounts under 10 days old now score 2 points on join alone — enough to trigger an alert without any other signals present. Accounts under 3 days score 3, under 24 hours score 4. During a live stream the threshold drops to 1, meaning any single suspicious signal is enough to flag a join. All join alerts include account age, join date, and score prominently.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
/broadcast — Urgency Levels from Discord Mobile
The /broadcast slash command now supports urgency as a dropdown choice — 🌿 Chill for informational notices, 🕯 Burning for security concerns, and 🔥 ON FIRE for urgent alerts. Urgency controls the embed colour and title prefix. The dropdown works from Discord mobile, meaning broadcasts can be sent from anywhere without needing access to the Desk.
Jun 2026
Hauscall Desk
Mod Tracking — Per-Session Active / Lurking / Not Seen
A new Mods tab in the AetherhausDesk Streamer Hub tracks mod activity in real time during live streams. A static mod list is maintained per streamer — mods are marked Active if they have sent a message in the last 10 minutes, Lurking if they have been seen this session but gone quiet, and Not Seen if they haven't appeared yet. The tab auto-refreshes every 30 seconds while the stream is live. Mods can be added and removed directly from the tab.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
Metrics — Permanent Session Snapshots
Session metrics are now persisted indefinitely in a separate storage file, independent of the 14-day transcript cleanup. Every session end writes a compact snapshot — message count, flag counts, bits, subs, Throne contributions, donations, and Hype Train data. The Stream History Graph and Partner Health Report now draw from this permanent record first, meaning monthly reporting stays accurate regardless of how old the data is.

The bar chart is more readable at all session counts, shows all metrics by default, and supports the same toggle controls. Hover tooltips show exact values per bar.
