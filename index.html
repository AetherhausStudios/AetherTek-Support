The AetherTek Ethos

The issue with big tech is that it is robotic, impersonal, and exploitative. AetherTek is not interested in that approach.

Every ticket is read by a person. Every partnership is configured personally. Every commission is discussed openly before a single line is written or a penny is spent. There are no automated responses, no copy-paste replies, no hidden costs, and no disappearing after delivery.

Community protection comes first. Hauscall flags — humans decide. Automation assists but never replaces judgement. The people in these communities are real, and they deserve infrastructure that treats them that way.

Negotiations on commissions are not just tolerated — they are welcomed and encouraged. The goal is to find an arrangement that works for both parties without placing undue strain on the client. Respectful discussion is always met with equal respect.

Built from a boredom project. Grown into something worth building properly.
