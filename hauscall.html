How It Works

Step 1
You connect Hauscall to your Discord server. It begins monitoring in real-time for phishing, fraud, spam, and policy violations.
Step 2
Hauscall flags suspicious activity and sends alerts to your moderation channel. It does not take action automatically. You decide what to do.
Step 3
Use moderation commands (/resolve, /escalate, /ticket) to manage incidents. Hauscall helps you keep records and generate reports.
Step 4
At the end of each stream, Hauscall generates a transcript with chat history, events, and flagged content. Review it or discard it. The data is yours.

Commands

Queue Management
/queue Shows all open tickets in priority order
/ticket TKT-XXXXXX Shows full details of a specific ticket
Ticket Lifecycle
/reviewing TKT-XXXXXX Triggers the "I'm looking into this" DM
/resolve TKT-XXXXXX Marks ticket resolved, sends rating link to user
/escalate TKT-XXXXXX [urgency] Bumps ticket priority
/close TKT-XXXXXX [reason] Closes without resolution
Metrics & Insight
/rating Your average rating across all tickets
/rating TKT-XXXXXX Rating for a specific ticket
/history @handle All past reports and alerts for a user
/transcript username Search chat transcript for a Twitch username
Sentinel
/broadcast [message] [urgency] Sends a broadcast to all connected mod channels
/retract [reason] Retracts a previous broadcast
/clarify [message] Pipes a clarification request to the workspace
/suspicion [message_link] Full suspicion score breakdown on a message
Utility
/commands Shows this command list
/joke Tells a random tech or D&D joke

⊹ Terms of Service

What Hauscall Does

Hauscall is a Discord bot that monitors community health and assists human moderators. It is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment.

  • Detects phishing, scams, spam, and policy violations
  • Analyzes images for AI-generated content
  • Generates moderation alerts
  • Creates chat transcripts (14 days retention)

Limitations

Hauscall is not perfect. It will miss violations and flag false positives. AetherhausStudios is not liable for missed alerts or service disruptions.
