A running record of what's changed, improved, or been added to AetherTek and Hauscall. No version numbers, no patch notes — just honest updates on what's new and why.
June 2026
Jun 2026
Hauscall Moderation
AI Image Detection
Hauscall can now analyse images posted in watched Discord channels for signs of AI generation. Detection runs on contextual signals first — account age, filename patterns, caption language, image dimensions — and only calls Claude Vision when enough signals are present. Rate-limited to 20 analyses per day. Alerts post to the mod channel with the image inline, the verdict, and the reasoning. Hauscall flags, mods decide.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
Hype Train Tracking
Hauscall now detects Twitch Hype Trains via EventSub webhooks and records them per session — level reached, contribution count, and date. A leaderboard per streamer tracks their all-time best trains, sorted by highest level. The post-stream Discord report now includes Hype Train count and highest level for each session.
Jun 2026
Hauscall
Community Event Tracking
Bits cheered, subs, gifted subs, Throne contributions, and donations are now extracted directly from the Twitch chat stream during sessions and recorded against the session. The post-stream Discord report now includes a Community line showing these totals — bits and subs as counts, Throne and donations as occurrence counts only, no dollar amounts shown publicly.
Jun 2026
Support Site Commissions
Commission Page Rebuilt
The commission intake page now clearly outlines pricing tiers ($50 base, with Low / Medium / High complexity tiers), explains hosting costs, the 6-month support guarantee, and makes explicit that submitting a brief carries no obligation. Negotiation is actively encouraged — the page now says so directly.

Support Site
Status Page
A live status page now shows the operational state of Hauscall's subsystems in real time — bot connectivity, API, Twitch monitoring, Discord monitoring, and transcripts. Auto-refreshes every 60 seconds. Also shows the weekly public digest when available.
May 2026
Hauscall
Weekly Public Digest
Hauscall generates a weekly digest every Sunday at 10am CST summarising the previous week's activity — sessions monitored, messages captured, alerts fired. Visible on the Status page. No identifying information is included.
May 2026
Partnerships Commissions
Business Ticket System
Partnership and commission inquiries now go through a structured intake process with dedicated forms, reference IDs, and Hauscall DM updates at each stage — received, under review, accepted or declined. Every decision is communicated directly and explained honestly.

Hauscall
Cross-Server Broadcast System
Security alerts and community notices can now be broadcast simultaneously to all connected servers, with the ability to retract them cleanly across all channels if circumstances change. Every broadcast is logged.
Early 2026
Hauscall
Transcript Archive & Post-Stream Reports
Full chat transcripts are recorded for every monitored stream session and auto-posted to a configured Discord channel when the stream ends. Each transcript includes a moderation report flagging anything that needs a second look — confirmed violations and items for mod discretion clearly separated. Flags require mod review before any action is taken.
Early 2026
Hauscall
Hauscall Goes Live
Hauscall — the familiar of Aetherhaus, keeper of the queue — went live as the operational backbone of AetherhausStudios tech support. Starting with ticket management, support DMs, and a rating system, it has grown steadily from there. Hauscall is not a public bot. Access is extended to trusted servers by invitation from Aether.
