Tech Support & Hardware
How long does tech support take?
Response times vary depending on the hour of submission and current volume. Best results come from submitting between 9pm and midnight CST — that's when Aether is most reliably available for support sessions. Smaller issues and quick questions can often be handled earlier in the day. The scope of the issue also plays a role — a quick fix is a quick fix, but more complex problems naturally take more time to diagnose and resolve properly.
Is there a cost for tech support or hardware advice?
No cost is charged or expected for tech support or hardware advice. These services exist because this is what AetherTek does — helping people navigate their technology without making it painful. Tips are genuinely appreciated but entirely optional, and Aether asks only that you tip what you don't need.
What kinds of issues can Aether help with?
Device troubleshooting, streaming setup, OBS and Streamlabs problems, network issues, Steam and game optimisation, system performance, and general hardware advice. If something is broken, cursed, or deeply confusing — it's worth submitting. Aether will always be upfront about whether something falls outside what can be helped with.
Hauscall
What is Hauscall?
Hauscall is functionally a bot and a relay — operating in both the foreground and background to deliver information to the AetherTek dashboard, and in turn directly to partners. It handles ticket routing, DM communications, stream transcript logging, Discord channel monitoring, raid and bot detection, and several other moderation assist functions. Think of it as the infrastructure layer that keeps the operation running while Aether focuses on the people side of things.
Is Hauscall available as a public bot?
No. Hauscall is not a public bot and is not available for general server invite. Access is extended through the AetherTek Partnership to communities that are a good fit. If you're interested, the partnership application is the right place to start.
AetherTek Partnership
What does the AetherTek Partnership actually include?
The partnership encompasses both the streamer and their community. On the Twitch side: full session transcript logging with post-session moderation analysis calibrated to your community's specific rules. On the Discord side: channel monitoring for AI art, unsolicited promotion, and welfare concerns, plus real-time raid and bot detection with clickable account profiles sent directly to your mod channel. Partners also get access to Aether's tech support services directly within their community. Everything is configured personally — nothing is set-and-forget.
How does the moderation analysis work?
After each stream session, Hauscall reviews the full transcript against your community's specific ruleset. Confirmed flags are listed clearly. Borderline cases are flagged separately as worth a second look — with a clear note that no action is implied and mods retain full discretion. The system flags. Humans decide. Always.
Project Commissions
What happens after I submit a commission brief?
Your brief will be reviewed and given the proper attention to fully understand the project before Aether reaches out to discuss details. The brief is not the same as an agreement — nothing is committed to on either side until scope and cost have been discussed and agreed upon. Aether will always be upfront about whether a project is something that can be taken on, and will always explain if the answer is no.
How is pricing determined?
Pricing starts at a base of $50 with complexity tiers — simple single-purpose builds, multifunctional applications with API integrations, and full builds with backend hosting setup are each priced differently. Any additional costs (such as third-party services) are communicated transparently before any work begins. Negotiation is welcomed and encouraged — the goal is an arrangement that works for both parties without placing undue strain on the client. Hosting costs are the client's responsibility, though Aether will always point you toward an affordable option suited to your needs.
Can Aether decline a commission?
Yes. Aether reserves the right to decline a commission, and will always explain why. Common reasons include current work volume, project scope, or insufficient clarity in the brief. Declining is never personal, and the door is not permanently closed.
Privacy & Communication
How does AetherTek handle my information?
AetherTek prefers all communication to occur via Discord and is designed to avoid any and all personal identifying information. No email addresses are requested. No real names or location data are asked for or needed. Payment information cannot be retained — not that there is any interest in that information anyway. The only information collected is what you choose to share in the course of getting help.
I have a question that isn't answered here. What do I do?
Reach out via Discord. Aether is a person, not a support bot, and is happy to answer questions directly. If something keeps coming up, it'll find its way into this page.
