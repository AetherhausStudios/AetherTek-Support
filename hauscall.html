An Exclusive Partnership with AetherTek by AetherhausStudios
Hauscall does not knock on just anyone's door. The AetherTek partnership is extended to trusted communities within Aether's network — servers whose admins understand that good tech support and community safety aren't luxuries. What follows is everything that comes with it.
🔧
AetherTek Support Services
Real Tech Help. No Guesswork.

Your community gets direct access to Aether's tech support pipeline — the same one that has untangled streaming setups, exorcised cursed hardware, and dragged more than a few devices back from the brink. Tickets are handled in order, with care, and at no cost to your members.

🎟️
Full Ticket Submission Access
Your members can submit support tickets directly via the AetherTek support page — covering device issues, streaming chaos, OBS afflictions, network untangling, Steam and game optimisation, and the general miscellaneous disaster.
🔩
Hardware Recommendations on a Budget
Monitors, keyboards, microphones, webcams, streaming peripherals, PC components — Aether hunts down quality within your members' budgets so they don't have to wade through the endless sea of conflicting opinions themselves.
📬
Hauscall Automated DM Updates
Members who provide their Discord User ID and opt in receive automated DMs at every stage of their support journey — ticket received, review begun, and resolution confirmed. They always know where they stand.
🛡️
Community Security
Alerts Before the Damage Is Done.

Phishing attempts, compromised accounts, and bad actors don't wait for a convenient moment. Hauscall's Sentinel network means your mod team hears about threats before they reach your members — with enough detail to act on it immediately.

⚠️
Security Broadcasts via Hauscall
Verified alerts about compromised accounts, phishing campaigns, and active threats are pushed directly to your designated mod channel the moment they're confirmed — automatically, across every server in the network simultaneously.
🔍
Verified Before It Reaches You
Nothing gets broadcast without Aether's review. No panic, no speculation — only confirmed, actionable information. Your mod team gets the facts, not the noise.
📣
The Clarification Pipeline
Your mods can use Hauscall's /clarify command to pipe questions about any alert directly to Aether for review and response. A line of communication that bypasses the noise.
↩️
Clean Retractions When Needed
If an alert requires correction or retraction, Hauscall handles it cleanly across every connected server — no ambiguity, no lingering confusion.
📡
Twitch Stream Monitoring
Eyes on the Stream. Always.

For streamers in the partnership, Hauscall monitors your live chat in real time — watching for mentions of Aether so tech issues get flagged the moment they're raised, not twenty minutes later when the stream has already suffered for it.

🟢
Live Status Monitoring
Hauscall tracks when you go live and when you go offline, keeping Aether's dashboard current without any manual input required on your end.
💬
Real-Time Chat Listening
Hauscall watches your live chat for mentions of Aether — by name, handle, or common variations. The moment someone calls for tech support mid-stream, Aether is notified. Viewer complaints don't get buried in chat.
📝
Full Chat Transcription
Every stream session is transcribed in full — every message, timestamped, attributed, and stored. This isn't a highlight reel. It is the complete record.
🗂️
14-Day Retention, No Questions Asked
Transcripts are retained for fourteen days from the date of the stream. If a viewer complaint surfaces after the fact, if a mod needs to verify what was said, if you simply want the record — it is there. Request it from Aether directly and it will be delivered personally.
⟡ A Note on Transcript Requests

When you need a transcript, you don't file a ticket or navigate a system. You message Aether directly. This is intentional. Chat logs can carry sensitive content — viewer disputes, harassment incidents, things that deserve discretion. Aether handles every transcript request personally, delivers it securely, and treats the contents with the confidentiality they deserve. The fourteen-day window exists not as a bureaucratic constraint, but as a practical one. Within it, the record is yours to call upon — no justification required, no questions asked.

How Hauscall Works in Your Server
01
One channel. That's all it needs.
Hauscall asks for access to a single mod-only channel in your server. Security broadcasts, clarification requests, and any Hauscall activity goes there — and only there. It does not post to public channels, it does not moderate, and it does not interfere with your existing setup.
02
Your members are already covered.
Because Hauscall lives in your server, it already shares that space with your community. Members who provide their Discord User ID when submitting a ticket and opt into notifications will receive Hauscall's automated DMs automatically — no additional steps, no separate server to join, nothing extra required on their end.
03
Everything else runs automatically.
Security alerts arrive when threats are confirmed. Transcripts run in the background during every stream. Tech support tickets flow through the pipeline. Your team doesn't manage any of it — Hauscall and Aether handle it between them.
04
Access is by invitation only. That's the point.
Hauscall is not a public bot. This partnership is offered to servers within Aether's trusted community — people Aether already knows, communities that align with the values that make this worth doing. If you're reading this, you likely already know how to reach out.
