Hauscall

Familiar of Aetherhaus · Keeper of the Queue

Composed. Dutiful. Probably Fine.

Hauscall is the familiar that keeps the Aetherhaus support operation running. It does not possess Aether's power — but it operates in Aether's name, carries Aether's authority, and manages the structured touchpoints so that every petitioner knows where they stand.
How It Works
📋
Step 1 — Submission
You submit a ticket
Via the support page. Hauscall receives it, logs it to the queue, and sends you a confirmation DM if you opted in.
Greetings, traveller. Hauscall here, familiar of Aetherhaus and keeper of the queue.

Your support ticket {TKT-XXXXXX} has been received, logged, and is currently {X} in line. Aether will be with you as soon as the chaos permits — you will be notified the moment your case enters review.

Should your situation worsen or your urgency change, feel free to resubmit. The queue is watched.

Sit tight, and do try not to panic.
🔍
Step 2 — Review
Aether begins reviewing your case
When Aether picks up your ticket, Hauscall lets you know — so you're ready when contact comes.
Greetings again, traveller. Hauscall, returning with news.

Aether has begun reviewing your support ticket {TKT-XXXXXX}. You can expect a direct message shortly to gather any additional information needed and begin working with you towards resolution.

The gears are turning. Sit tight.
✦
Step 3 — Resolution
Your ticket is marked resolved
Hauscall sends a final message with a link to leave a rating and, if you're so inclined, a tip — on your terms entirely.
Greetings, traveller. Hauscall, returning one final time.

Your support ticket {TKT-XXXXXX} has been marked as resolved. It is hoped that Aether's assistance has brought you closer to resolution, or at the very least, eased the burden of getting there.

If you found the assistance helpful, a moment of your time to leave a rating would be most appreciated — five stars, if the chaos was managed to your satisfaction. You may do so here: {link}

A note on tips: Aether does not ask for them. They are entirely optional. Should you consider leaving one, Aether asks only that you tip what you do not need — this assistance is about your wellbeing and peace of mind, first and foremost.

Should chaos, device possession, software revolutions, or the odd web-based curse rear its ugly head once again — you know where to find us.
Command Reference
Ticket Lifecycle
/reviewing TKT-XXXXXX Triggers the review begun DM to the submitter
/resolve TKT-XXXXXX Marks resolved, sends the resolution DM with rating link
/escalate TKT-XXXXXX Bumps a ticket to higher urgency in the queue
/close TKT-XXXXXX [reason] Closes without resolution — no rating link sent, reason logged
Queue Management
/queue Shows current open tickets in order with urgency and position
/ticket TKT-XXXXXX Pulls full details of a specific ticket
Metrics
/rating Shows your current average rating across all reviews
/rating TKT-XXXXXX Shows the rating for a specific resolved ticket
/history @handle Pulls all past reports and alerts for a Discord handle
Sentinel
/broadcast [message] Sends a security alert to all connected mod channels
/retract @handle Retracts a previous broadcast cleanly across all servers
/clarify @handle Mod command — pipes a clarification request to the workspace
Utility
/commands Displays this full command list in channel
