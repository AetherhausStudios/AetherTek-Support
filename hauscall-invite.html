Free & Exclusive Partnership with AetherhausStudios Tech Support
Hauscall is the familiar that manages the AetherhausStudios tech support operation. Adding it to your server gives your community direct access to Aether's support services — and gives your moderation team a direct line to community security alerts.
What Hauscall Brings to Your Server
🎟️
Tech Support Access

Your members can submit tech support tickets directly to Aether — hardware issues, streaming chaos, OBS afflictions, network untangling and more.

📬
Automated DM Updates

Hauscall sends your members direct messages at every stage of their support journey — ticket received, review begun, and resolution confirmed.

⚠️
Security Broadcasts

During Phishing Season and beyond, Hauscall delivers verified security alerts about compromised accounts directly to your mod team — before they become your server's problem.

🔍
Clarification Pipeline

Your mods can use /clarify to pipe questions about any alert directly to Aether for review and response.

What Your Community Gets
✦
Priority access to Aether's tech support queue

Your members submit tickets via the AetherhausStudios support page and enter the same queue as everyone else — handled in order, with care, at no cost.

✦
Hardware recommendations on a budget

Monitors, keyboards, mics, streaming peripherals — Aether hunts down quality within your members' budgets so they don't have to.

✦
Proactive community security coverage

When compromised accounts are spotted across the network, your mod team hears about it first — with enough detail to act before it spreads.

✦
A direct line to someone who knows what they're doing

Aether has been solving tech problems across streaming communities for years. Your members get that expertise without the guesswork.

Setup Requirements
Give Hauscall access to one mod-only channel — security broadcasts and clarification requests will be posted there, keeping alerts out of public view.
Hauscall does not post to public channels, does not moderate, and does not interfere with your existing bots or server structure.
Access is by invitation only — Hauscall is extended to servers within Aether's trusted community.
